Driveway Re-Levelling

Home > Residential Solutions > Driveway Re-Levelling

Restore levels, remove trip hazards, and preserve your driveway appearance cleanly and with minimal disruption.

Slab driveways can settle or heave when soils change volume, lose bearing, or are mobilised through water ingress. Our targeted ground improvement and controlled re-levelling supports the slab and lifts it back toward design levels, often without removing the driveway or shutting down access for long periods. We inject engineered resin beneath the slab to fill voids, densify weak soils, and apply measured lift, with outcomes verified in real time.

Related Service: Foundation Repair

Why Driveways Move

  • Reactive soil cycles: swelling, shrinking, and differential movement.
  • Uncontrolled fill or poor compaction leading to long-term consolidation.
  • Erosion or washout around downpipes, irrigation lines, or unsealed panel joints.
  • Traffic and point loads from cars, boats, trailers, and over-loaded weak spots.

Related Problems: Ground Subsidence

How Driveway Re-Levelling Works

01

Assess & Map

We survey levels, locate voids or loose zones, and define injection points away from edges and services.

02

Targeted Resin Injection

Small holes are drilled and a high-strength expanding resin is injected at appropriate depths beneath each panel. The resin fills voids, binds loose material, increases bearing capacity, and can gently lift the slab back toward level.

Related Service: Ground Stabilise

03

Controlled Lift & Checks

Lift is applied in micro-increments and monitored continuously for levels, joints, falls to drains, and interfaces.

Related Service: Slab Lifting

04

Finish & Verify

Holes are plugged, joints can be sealed, and before/after levels are recorded. Where needed, we advise on drainage improvements to prevent recurrence.

Benefits

Non-destructive

Keep the existing slab with no bulk demolition.

Fast return to service

Resin cures quickly; typical areas are usable the same day.

Precise

Millimetre-scale control to restore falls and remove trip edges.

Clean worksite

Small injection points, minimal mess, and minimal waste.

Limitations & Suitability

The slab may be too broken to act monolithically and lift together, including multiple full-depth crack fragments, shattered corners, or delamination.

Treatment width may be too narrow to correct gradient adequately, especially slab edge panels or differential recovery.

Existing falls, pits, thresholds, or crossover levels may limit how much correction can be achieved without creating a new drainage issue.

Severe root heave is the primary cause. Resin will not counter ongoing tree growth forces.

We perform a level survey to confirm the priority of movement, review any settlement, and the extent of vertical change so you know if ground remediation can realistically restore alignment.

Cost & Decision Factors

Small, localised sections are often cheaper to replace than to remediate because resin work has a fixed setup cost and mobilisation. Larger areas or multiple panels tend to favour re-levelling, especially where demolition/reinstatement would be disruptive or expensive.

Finish Matters

Exposed aggregate or stencilled concrete is difficult to match in a partial replacement. Re-levelling preserves the original look.

Thresholds overlay may not be replaceable or available, making re-levelling more sensitive to finish.

Functionality considerations

If settlement has upset drainage, re-levelling can restore falls without a full rebuild.

Typical Issues We Fix

  • Sunken panels creating trip steps at garage entries, thresholds, or path interfaces.
  • Loss of falls to drains causing ponding.
  • Void pumping and slab drumming over soft spots.
  • Settled edges at crossovers and vehicle pads.
